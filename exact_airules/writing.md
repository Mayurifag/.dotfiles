# Writing

- Apply this rule to authored prose, including replies, documentation, comments, and UI copy. Match the audience,
  language, purpose, and existing voice; prefer concrete details and natural phrasing.
- Remove empty introductions and conclusions, generic praise, inflated claims, bureaucratic or sales jargon, and filler.
  Avoid buzzwords, formulaic contrasts, repetitive transitions or sentence patterns, forced lists, and decorative
  punctuation.
- In Russian, prefer finite verbs to participial and adverbial-participial phrases; avoid excessive verbal nouns. Keep
  these constructions where they are natural or help precision. Apply the same judgment to nominalizations in other
  languages; do not treat individual words or grammar as proof of AI authorship.
- Before sending or saving prose, check for these patterns. Preserve meaning, technical terms, quotations, requested
  formats, and useful detail; do not invent facts or flatten the voice to sound human.
