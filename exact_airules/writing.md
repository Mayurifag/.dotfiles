# Writing

- Match audience, language, purpose, and existing voice; use concrete details and natural phrasing.
- Remove empty introductions/conclusions, generic praise, inflated claims, bureaucratic/sales jargon, filler, buzzwords,
  formulaic contrasts, repetitive transitions/patterns, forced lists, and decorative punctuation.
- In Russian, prefer finite verbs over participial/adverbial-participial phrases and excessive verbal nouns, unless
  natural or more precise. Use the same judgment for nominalizations elsewhere; words/grammar do not prove AI
  authorship.
- Check before sending/saving. Preserve meaning, technical terms, quotations, requested formats, and useful detail;
  never invent facts or flatten the voice to sound human.
